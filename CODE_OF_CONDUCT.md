# Code of conduct

Project Ambrose is built by people from many places, many of them working through an AI assistant. The same standard applies to all of them, in the repository, on Discord and on r/ProjectAmbrose.

## Expected

- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Judge the work, not the person, and say how a claim can be checked.
- Assume good faith, and ask before accusing.
- Credit other people's work, and never pass off someone else's finding as your own.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing game files, cheats, stolen accounts, or material that breaks KingsIsle Entertainment's terms, in any project space.
- Flooding the review queue with work made only to look like a contribution.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can remove comments, close pull requests, and block people from the repository and the community spaces. Report a problem privately to the maintainer on Discord, or through the repository's Security tab if it involves a security issue. Reports are kept confidential.
